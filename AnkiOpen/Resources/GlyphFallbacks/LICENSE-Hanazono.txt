Glyph fallback PNGs in this folder were generated from Hanazono Mincho
Version 8.030 (HanaMinB.otf and HanaMinC.otf), maintained by the cjkvi
project and created from GlyphWiki data.

Source:
https://github.com/cjkvi/HanaMinAFDKO

The upstream project states that its license follows the GlyphWiki license:
https://glyphwiki.org/wiki/GlyphWiki:License

Only the small PNG fallback glyphs needed by this app's current corpus are
bundled here; the full Hanazono font files are not included.
